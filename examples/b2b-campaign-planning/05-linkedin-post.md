# LinkedIn Post — B2B Campaign Planning

## Post copy

Before you pick a campaign theme, there are a few things you need to get right first.

The two inputs that matter most: product marketing (who you're targeting, their pain points, your competitive positioning) and brand marketing (the direction everything needs to ladder up to). You need both before the brainstorm starts — not during it.

Once you have them, you can test a theme against a simple standard: a good campaign theme isn't a tagline. It's a point of view that challenges an assumption your buyers currently hold. It works in paid media, organic social, and email without becoming a different campaign in each place. And it's ownable — something you can say that your competitors genuinely can't.

The last step that gets skipped most often: bring everyone into the brainstorm before anything is decided. Product marketing, brand, content, creative, copywriting. When people shape the theme from the start, the work that comes out of each team naturally pulls in the same direction.

This applies whether you're doing B2B or B2C. The channels are different. The logic isn't.
